About Our Parish

Our Lady of the Assumption Syro-Malabar Catholic Mission is a vibrant and growing faith community located in Fairfield, dedicated to serving the faithful throughout Southern Connecticut. As a proud member of the Syro-Malabar Catholic Church—one of the 23 Eastern Catholic Churches in full communion with the Holy See—our mission is a living testament to the rich liturgical and spiritual heritage of the ancient Chaldean rite. We provide a spiritual home for those seeking to preserve these venerable traditions while fostering a close-knit community of believers. Whether you are deeply rooted in the Syro-Malabar tradition or looking to experience the beauty of Eastern Catholicism, we invite you to join us in worship, service, and fellowship as we grow together in Christ.

Our parish family gathers every Sunday to celebrate the Holy Qurbana (Mass) together, rooted in the traditions of Saint Thomas the Apostle who brought Christianity to India. We warmly welcome all Syro Malabar Catholics and anyone who wishes to explore our beautiful faith tradition.
